Abby Knott • Interior Designer
The Designer Behind The Space

Rooted in South Saint Paul, designing for human experience.

Abby Knott holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Interior Design from the University of Wisconsin–Stout. Her background encompasses multi-disciplinary design—ranging from complex public museum concepts and workplace space planning to custom residential remodels and furniture design.

She views interior design not as superficial decoration, but as the thoughtful synthesis of volume, light, function, and emotional atmosphere. Every project begins with a deep respect for structural conditions and how people inhabit space.

Core Principles
01

PEOPLE

Design begins and ends with the human beings who inhabit the space. Comfort, accessibility, and intuitive navigation guide every layout decision.

02

PURPOSE

Every spatial gesture, material selection, and lighting placement must serve a distinct functional or emotional purpose. Nothing is visual filler.

03

DETAIL

Material honesty, subtle joint details, exact proportions, and rigorous construction documentation elevate concepts into durable realities.
